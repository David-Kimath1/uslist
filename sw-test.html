Update Flow — Test

A preview of how the "new version available" notification and loader would look inside the real app.

1. The Update Banner

Appears when a new version is detected. Bottom-right of the screen. Stays until the user either updates or dismisses it.

2. The Loader

Full-screen overlay that appears after clicking "Update". Blocks interaction, shows a pulsing heart, then refreshes.

3. Full Flow

Complete experience: banner appears → user clicks Update → loader shows → app reloads with new version.

Notes

This is just the visual demo. In the real app, the banner would appear automatically when a new service worker is detected. The loader would only show for ~1.5s then the page reloads.
